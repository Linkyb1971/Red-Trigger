Red Trigger for Windows (64-bit)

  Copy the "Red Trigger.vst3" folder to  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

  "Red Trigger.exe" is a standalone version you can run directly.

FIRST USE
  Red Trigger ships without sounds. Open the plugin, click "Instrument Builder",
  drop in a folder of drum one-shots and click "Save Instrument...".
  Instruments are stored in Documents\Music\Red Trigger\Instruments (your Music folder).
